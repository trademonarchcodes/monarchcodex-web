Online Cooperative

Learn About the Cooperative

This page explains the Online Cooperative, what members can expect, how packages and referrals fit into the programme, and how to create your cooperative account.

01 · What It Is

A Separate Cooperative Account

The Online Cooperative is a partner programme where members create their cooperative account through the official registration page. Your cooperative account is created and managed on the partner platform, separately from your MONARCH CODEX member account.

02 · Packages

Choose a Cooperative Package

The cooperative programme may offer different packages or participation levels. The available package details, requirements, pricing and terms are presented on the cooperative registration platform before you create your account.

03 · Referrals

Referral Programme

The cooperative programme can include referral-based participation. Your referral activity, qualification rules and any applicable rewards are determined by the cooperative platform and its current programme terms.

04 · Participation

How You Get Started

Start by opening the cooperative registration page, create your cooperative account, and review the information provided there. Follow the platform's instructions for completing your profile and participating in the programme.

05 · Your Account

Keep Your Accounts Separate

Your MONARCH CODEX login and your Online Cooperative account are separate accounts. Creating a cooperative account does not replace your MONARCH CODEX account or change your wallet, KYC, investment or withdrawal records here.

06 · Before You Join

Review the Terms

Read the cooperative platform's package information, referral rules, requirements, charges and other applicable terms carefully before registering or making any payment.
